_Think Global_ is a space for ideas that reach beyond local projects and look at the wider world: technology, science, politics, global trends, and the patterns shaping our future.

Here I gather reflections, analysis, and perspectives on how systems evolve, why certain changes happen, and what they might mean for all of us. These are not news updates or abstract theories — they’re attempts to make sense of the world and connect the dots between seemingly unrelated developments.

This category is about stepping back, zooming out, and exploring long-term processes, big questions, and small insights that help illuminate the bigger picture.
