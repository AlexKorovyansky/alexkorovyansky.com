# Intro

I’m a co-founder and CEO of Effective. We help companies turn technology and AI into real business results. Alongside consulting, I also teach, from corporate AI training to programs for students.

### Consulting

- **Fractional CTO.** Executive-level technology leadership on a flexible basis: technology strategy, architecture, and team scaling for startups and growing companies, at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.
- **Fractional CAIO.** Executive-level AI leadership on a flexible basis: AI strategy, identifying high-impact use cases, and guiding adoption across the organization, without the cost of a full-time Chief AI Officer.
- **Forward Deployed Engineers.** Senior engineers embedded within client teams to accelerate delivery and resolve complex technical challenges.

### Education

- **AI Challenge.** An intensive corporate program by Effective that equips professionals to integrate AI into their daily workflows and boost productivity.
- **Projectim.** A nonprofit I founded to develop the next generation of tech talent through real-world, team-based projects and mentorship.
- **Career in Tech.** A pro bono lecture course for first-year university students on building a successful career in IT.

### Thought leadership

In the age of AI, what matters most is meaning. As machines take on more of the how, the question of why becomes ours to answer. In the Think Global section of this site, I publish essays on a wide range of topics that I find important and worth exploring, written for anyone curious about the world we’re building.
